# Saudi / Riyadh car retail: market context fact base

Compiled 2026-10-18 by web research, to give context to a field census of independent showrooms (sub-dealers) in Al Qadisiyah (east Riyadh, Dammam Rd / Exit 8) and Al Shifa (south Riyadh).

**Confidence key.** High: primary or official source (GASTAT, SAMA via press, ministry), read directly. Medium: reputable press or tracker, read directly, with some definitional ambiguity. Low: commercial research estimate, blog or directory, or a figure seen only in a search snippet.

"Derived" means a ratio I calculated from the source's own numbers. The source does not state it.

## How this could contextualise a two-district census

- **Scale anchor.** About 0.83-0.86m new light vehicles were sold nationally in 2024-25. Riyadh is roughly a quarter of national demand: 26.0% of 2024 new registrations, 26.7% of the 2022 population (Riyadh Region), and 37.1% of first-time licences. That implies Riyadh demand on the order of ~210-230k new vehicles per year (rough, derived). A census of ~55+ Qadisiyah showrooms plus the Al Shifa strip can be framed against this, for example via observed stock counts and turnover estimates per showroom. Be explicit that a census measures stock and outlets, not sales.
- **Channel context.** The 2022 Ministry of Commerce measures explicitly targeted agent-to-showroom reselling. That is the supply route for Qadisiyah's "new non-agent" stock, so census questions on sourcing (agent allocation, GCC re-export, own import) and on price premiums over agent list price are well grounded. Note that 84% of new registrations are to establishments, so fleet and rental flows matter.
- **Used side.** Used transactions may run ~1.9x new (Ken estimate, low confidence), and the used market is majority informal or offline (~59-70%). An Al Shifa census adds rare primary data on the "unorganised" segment that consultancies only estimate.
- **Finance.** SAMA data shows finance-company auto lending is flat at ~SR25-26bn (~26% of their book) from 2024 to Q2 2025. Asking showrooms what share of deals are financed, and through which lender (bank Ijara vs finance company vs in-house instalments), would test the unverified "~65-70% financed" claims.
- **Brand mix.** Toyota (~28-29%) and Hyundai (~15-16%) dominate. Chinese brands are at ~12% (H1 2025) and possibly rising (~16% in May 2026, unverified), while Changan and MG fell in 2025 and Jetour and Haval surged. Census brand counts per lot can be compared against these national shares to show whether independents over-index on Chinese or Japanese stock.
- **Timing caveat.** Focus2move snippets suggest a ~20% YoY drop in 2026 YTD sales (unverified). If true, the census is happening in a downturn, which is relevant to inventory and discount observations.
- **Spatial history.** In 2018 the Al Naseem haraj was relocated to Al Rimal (Tatweer auction) on congestion grounds. Showroom clusters in Riyadh are therefore subject to planning action, which supports recording lot size, parking and street-frontage use.
